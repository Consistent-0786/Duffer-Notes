# NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology)

- NIST is a non-regulatory agency which is a type of government agency that does not have the authority to enforce regulations or laws. Non-regulatory agencies typically provide guidance, recommendations, and best practices in their areas of expertise
- In Simple : 
	- A government organization that helps organizations implement and improve security through guidelines, frameworks, and best practices

## NIST Framework Types

- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**
- **NIST 800-37: Risk Management Framework**
- **NIST 800-53: Security and Privacy Controls**
- **NIST 800-61: Computer Security Incident Handling Guide**
- NIST 800-30: Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments
- NIST 800-160: Developing Cyber-Resilient Systems (Systems Security Engineering)

==NOTE : The Frameworks that are **bold** are important and are cover in Cyber-Range==
