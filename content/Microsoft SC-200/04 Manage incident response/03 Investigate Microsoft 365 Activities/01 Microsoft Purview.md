# Microsoft Purview Definition

- **Microsoft Purview** is a tool that helps organizations **protect, govern, and stay compliant with their data**, no matter **where the data lives** (on-prem, cloud, SaaS)

- It has **Three main areas** :
	1. **Data Security :**  
	    - Protects data from leaks and misuse  
	    - For Example : DLP, encryption, information protection, insider risk management
    
	2. **Data Governance :**  
	    - Helps you **understand and manage** your data  
	    - For Example : data discovery, classification, and management across systems like Azure, Power BI, Microsoft 365, Amazon We Services, etc
    
	3. **Risk & Compliance :**  
	    - Helps meet **laws and regulations**  
	    ForeDiscovery, compliance manager, data lifecycle management

> All of this is managed from **one central Purview portal**
👉 For **SC-200**, the focus is mainly on **Data Security**, especially **DLP** and **Insider Risk Management**
