# What are Sensitivity Labels ?

- **Sensitivity labels** are used to **classify and protect data** as it moves across users, devices, apps, and services **inside and outside** the organization for business collaboration
- They help keep data secure **without blocking collaboration**

## Why Sensitivity Labels are Needed

- Data no longer stays behind a firewall    
- Files and emails roam across **devices, cloud apps, and external users**    
- Labels ensure data stays **protected and compliant wherever it goes**    

## What Sensitivity Labels Can Do

- Sensitivity labels can :
	1. **Protect Content :**
	- **Encrypt** emails, documents, and meeting invites
    - **Restrict actions** (view, edit, print, forward)
	
	2. **Mark Content :**
	- Add **watermarks**, **headers**, and **footers**
	- For eg : _“Confidential”_ watermark on a document
    
## Where Sensitivity Labels Applied 

- They can be applied to :
	- **Office apps**: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook (desktop & web)    
	- **Microsoft Teams, M365 Groups, SharePoint sites**    
	- **Meetings and chats**    
	- **Power BI**    
	- **Purview Data Map** (SQL, Azure, AWS databases)    
	- **Third-party apps** (Salesforce, Box, Dropbox) via Defender for Cloud Apps    
	- **Custom apps** via Microsoft Information Protection SDK    
	- **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (Copilot respects labels)    

## Key Characteristics of Sensitivity Labels

- **Customizable** – Based on your organization’s security policy    
- **Clear text** – Stored as readable metadata    
- **Persistent** – Stays with the file wherever it’s stored
- **Policy-driven** – Used to enforce security rules

## What Sensitivity Labels Control

- **Access control** (via encryption)    
- **Content markings** (watermarks, headers, footers)    
- **Container settings** (Teams, sites, groups)    
- **Automatic or recommended labeling**    
- **Default sharing links** for SharePoint and OneDrive    

- **Example :**
	- Common label structure :
		- Public    
		- General    
		- Confidential    
		- Highly Confidential    

# Sub-Labels

- Labels can have **sublabels** **(eg= Confidential → All Employees)**    
- Sublabels :    
    - Help organize labels        
    - Do **not inherit settings** (except color)        
    - Are the actual labels users apply

> **In Simple :**
	- **Sensitivity labels classify and protect data**    
	- They apply **encryption, markings, and access rules**    
	- Labels are **persistent and policy-based**    
	- They work across **Microsoft 365, third-party apps, and Copilot**
