# What is Microsoft Purview Auditing?

- **Microsoft Purview Auditing** helps organizations **track, investigate, and respond to security and compliance events**
## What It Does

- Records **thousands of user and admin actions**
- Covers **many Microsoft services and solutions**
- Stores everything in a **single, unified audit log**
## Who Uses It

- Audit logs can be searched by :
	- Security operations teams    
	- IT administrators    
	- Insider risk teams    
	- Compliance and legal investigators
## Why It’s Important

- Provides **visibility into user and admin activities**
- Supports **forensic and internal investigations**
- Helps meet **compliance and legal requirements**

> In Simple :
	- Purview Auditing gives you a **complete activity trail across your organization to investigate incidents and prove compliance**
