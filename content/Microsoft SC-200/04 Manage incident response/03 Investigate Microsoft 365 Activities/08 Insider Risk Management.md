# What is Insider Risk Management ?

- **Insider Risk Management** helps organizations **detect, investigate, and reduce risks caused by internal users**, whether actions are **malicious or accidental**
- It focuses on risks that come from **employee behavior**, not external attackers

## Why Insider Risk Management Is Needed

- Not all risks come from outside, Internal risks include :
	- Data leaks or data spillage    
	- Confidentiality violations    
	- Fraud or insider trading    
	- Regulatory compliance violations    
	- Theft of intellectual property    

## What Purview Insider Risk Management Does

- Identifies **risky user activities**    
- Correlates multiple signals to detect risk    
- Helps **investigate and respond** to incidents    
- Allows escalation to **eDiscovery Premium**

## Insider Risk Policies

- Define **which risks to monitor** (IP theft, data leakage, security violations)  
- Create **alerts and cases**
- Guide analysts on **next actions**

## Key Capabilities

- Detects both **intentional and unintentional** insider threats   
- Uses signals from user activity across Microsoft 365
- Helps analysts take action to **enforce compliance**

## Privacy & Security by Design

- Users are **pseudonymized by default**    
- **Role-based access control (RBAC)** limits access    
- All actions are **audited**

> **In Simple** :
> 	- **Insider Risk Management targets internal threats**    
	- Detects **risky user behavior**    
	- Helps **investigate, respond, and escalate**    
	- Built with **privacy and compliance in mind**
